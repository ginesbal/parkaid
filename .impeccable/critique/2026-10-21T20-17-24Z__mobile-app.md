## Anti-patterns verdict

PASS. Nobody would assume at a glance that AI made this: one cerulean accent, the prussian ink canvas used with purpose, real Calgary zones and ParkPlus language, whispered shadows, and a custom 3D brand mark kept to Welcome and loading. No gradients, glass, glow or neon.

Template fingerprints a trained eye still finds on the working screens:
- A 3 pt cerulean rail on the selected Map list row (one-side coloured border).
- Details fact rows as stock "icon tile, bold title, muted sentence" feature rows.
- Numbered step badges (1, 2, 3) on a three-field form in Park setup.
- Cards in cards: grey summary box and grey inputs inside the white setup card; grey "Closest to you" card inside the white sheet.
- The stock centred empty state (big glyph, heading, body, solid button, outline button).

## Overall impression

The craft is real where it counts: the session card, the honest summary before Start, the capped durations, and the ink canvas are better than most portfolio apps. What lets it down is the gap between what the screens promise and what the app does: a warning that never reaches a locked phone, a priced Start that reads like a payment, error copy from the network layer, and on web a search field that leads to a map with no search. The single biggest opportunity: make the money moments as trustworthy as they look.

## What's working

1. The running session: "59 min" over "Ends at 2:52 PM", one priced primary ("Extend 30 min · $3.00") and a separate outline "End session". The warning recolours only the number, bar and one walk-aware line.
2. Honest money moments up to the tap: rate basis, zone, posted maximum and total before Start; durations capped at the maximum; unknown rates say "Check posted signs" instead of inventing a number.
3. Colour discipline: one accent, ink for focus screens, amber and brick only for urgency. Selection never relies on colour alone (checks, labels).
4. Real accessibility groundwork: radio roles, spoken row labels with prices, progress bar values, Extend announced, Reduce Motion honoured everywhere including the 3D pin.

## Priority issues

1. **[P1] Warnings never leave the app.** No expo-notifications, no AppState. "Time to head back" exists only inside the Park tab's card. A driver who locks the phone is never warned. Fix: schedule local notifications on Start and every Extend (at the walk-aware warning time and at the end), cancel on End; ask permission on first Start with a one-line reason. Command: /harden
2. **[P1] Start and Extend look like paying; nothing pays the City.** "Pay at zone 1684" sits above a cerulean "Start session"; the session card reads like a receipt; the only disclaimer is below the fold and a 12 pt "DEMO" tag. The Park tab's own demo asks for an hourly rate ($1.50/$2.50/$3.50) and records "Meter 217". Fix: one warningInk line above Start ("Demo. This does not pay the City. Pay in ParkPlus, zone 1684."), the same on the session card; replace the demo rate picker with "Where are you parked?" (closest spots from the last fetch). Command: /clarify
3. **[P1] Error states leak developer strings.** "HTTP 500" / "Failed to fetch" / "signal is aborted without reason" appear twice (status line and body), under a Wi-Fi glyph in danger red, for a server problem. Fix: classify offline / server / timeout, never render err.message, use a neutral glyph, and say what the driver can do. Command: /clarify
4. **[P1] "Near me" may not be near.** useLocationManager returns the stored location and never refreshes; pull to refresh refetches spots only; no service-area check. Fix: fresh fix on launch, focus and refresh; label stored or default locations; outside downtown, say so and show downtown. Command: /harden
5. **[P1] On web, "Search Calgary" leads to a map with no search.** It is the first thing a reviewer taps after "Where to today?". The web map ignores focusSearch. Fix: make search work on web (Places key exists) or render the field disabled with "Search runs on iOS and Android". Command: /adapt

Also confirmed (P2): the identical drawn map tile as the Details hero; placeholder plate that looks entered; six selected-state styles and accent on passive numbers; unpriced pins indistinct and 30 pt targets; "8 places within 250 m" listing spots at 260 to 470 m (one pin off screen); a weak ending (expired says it five ways, then a blank form); permit-only blocks mixed in with parkable spots; Extend without the new end time or undo; drag-only sheet with a faint handle; a running session invisible outside the Park tab; prices ignore charging hours; 8 s loader then silent stale data; two contrast misses (expired text 4.42:1, amber icon 3.13:1).

## Persona red flags

- **Casey (distracted, one thumb):** never warned once the phone is locked; reopens on Home with no sign of the running session; 8 s loader on weak signal, then a list of unknown age; top-pick card body is a dead tap area; recenter fades but still takes taps. Risk: a ticket.
- **Jordan (first-timer):** reads "Start · 1 h · $6.00" as paying; the Park tab asks for a rate they cannot know; errors blame their Wi-Fi; "Pan or zoom the map to search elsewhere" does nothing on native. Risk: believes ParkAid paid.
- **Sam (screen reader):** the map sheet opens only by dragging an unlabelled handle; recenter has no label; web pin labels read raw City strings ("6 Av SW , Fr 8 St SW To 9 St SW"); "Ends soon" / "Time's up" are not announced on iOS; expired text 4.42:1. Risk: cannot open the list or hear that time is nearly up.
- **Portfolio reviewer (60 seconds):** impressed by Welcome, the ink Details, the honest summary. Reads as unfinished: the same drawn map on every Details page, the drawn web map, invented rates and "Meter 217", "HTTP 500" twice when the backend sleeps, "00 Block 6 AV SW" as the first row. Risk: strong craft read as a student project.
- **Calgary driver, winter, gloves, late:** "Near me" from the wrong place; the $3 spot at the same walk ranks below the $6 one and the map list is in API order; prices quoted outside charging hours; no "copy zone" or "open ParkPlus"; 30 pt pins and 29 pt native chips. Risk: pays for free time or misses the warning.

## Minor observations

- The greeting is the biggest type on Home and carries no information; the 52 pt header pin adds weight.
- Type scale drift: about 13 sizes in use against 5 tokens; prices at 600 though tokens say 500; a spot name is 18, 16, 15 or 17 pt depending on the screen.
- Terms and casing drift: spot / place / parking; "2 h maximum" vs "2 hour maximum"; "/ hour" vs "/hr"; "View Map" in Title Case; "DEMO" uppercase.
- Raw City data in names and screen reader labels ("00 Block 6 AV SW", "455 6th St SW").
- The Spline badge outweighs the 72 pt loader pin on the first-load screen; give the pin more mass rather than touching the badge.
- The location dot colour (#2F7DF6) is hard-coded; make it a token.
