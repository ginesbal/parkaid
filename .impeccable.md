## Design Context

### Users
Primary audience right now: portfolio reviewers and recruiters judging the work (a SAIT capstone, redesigned for a portfolio). They meet it in a demo, the web build or screenshots, so first impressions, polish and a coherent story weigh heavily.

Design persona: drivers, commuters and downtown visitors in Calgary finding parking while on the move. Use is glanceable, time-sensitive and one-handed, often outdoors and sometimes on a weak connection. The job: find a spot they can afford near where they are going, understand its rules (rate, when it is charged, maximum stay, ParkPlus zone), start a session, and get back before time runs out.

So every screen has to work both as a portfolio screenshot and as a real tool on the street. Demos use real City of Calgary data and real places, never placeholder text or invented locations.

### Brand Personality
Calm, dependable, street-smart.

Voice: plain, short and local. Say what happens next. No hype, no emoji, no exclamation marks. Write times and limits in full ("Ends at 4:30 PM", "2 hour maximum"). When time is short or something fails, be direct and kind, never alarming.

Emotional goal: confidence and relief ("A little less circling. A little more city."). The 3D pin carries the personality in brand moments; working screens stay quiet.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Reference: Apple Maps. Map-forward, calm materials, glanceable cards, clear hierarchy, sheets rather than modals.
- Theme: light mode on #F7F8FA. Details and the running session sit on the prussian ink canvas (#0F1A26) with a white card. No full dark mode for now.
- Colour: one accent, cerulean #1d6d8b, for actions, selection and the pin. Tinted near-black ink for text, never pure black. Warm colour only for warnings (#B8832D, as text #8F6216) and danger (#B5524A). Hairline borders over heavy shadows.
- Type: the system sans in three weights (400 body, 500 numbers, 600 titles), with 700 only for the Welcome headline.
- Brand mark: the ParkAid pin from the Spline scene. On Welcome and the loading screens it is the live 3D scene (the jump and twirl from the file's Code tab) over a 3D still of the same pin. Everywhere else (headers, map markers, map thumbnails) it is the flat ParkAid logo. The free-plan "Built with Spline" badge stays visible in the screen's bottom-right corner; never hide, crop or cover it.
- Motion: short and purposeful (180 to 320 ms, ease-out, springs for sheets). Press scale 0.97. Entrances fade and rise 12 pt once. Reduce Motion swaps movement for opacity or stillness.
- Anti-references: the generic AI look (purple or rainbow gradients, glassmorphism, glowing cards, emoji, decorative blobs); marketing-landing redesigns; dense, ad-heavy parking apps and dashboard clutter; wrong or invented Calgary facts.

### Design Principles
1. Glanceable first: where, how much, how long and how far read in a second, with absolute times beside every countdown.
2. Honest at the money moments: show the total, end time, zone and limits before any tap that costs money. Never invent a price, and respect the posted maximum.
3. One accent, quiet surfaces: cerulean for action and selection, warm colour only for urgency, hairlines over shadows.
4. Personality in brand moments only: the 3D pin performs on Welcome and while loading; the working screens stay calm.
5. Presentable in every state: empty, loading, offline, expired and fallback states are designed, teach the next step, and look as good in a screenshot as the happy path. Accessible by default: WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, 44 pt touch targets, screen reader labels and Reduce Motion.
